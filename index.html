      How to play

      1. Ride through a square. Each ride earns a point in every 0.2-mile square it crosses.
      2. Close a loop. Earn a point in each interior square you surrounded on one ride. A one-square gap in the outline is allowed.
      3. Take control. The top score owns the square. Tied leaders share it, and each gets credit in the standings.
      4. Climb the boards. Compare squares you control now and the most squares you enclosed in a single ride.
